We were a construction company. Then we built our own AI layer. Now we are both.

A year ago Perennial Empire ran like every other 90-employee general contractor in New York. Estimating in spreadsheets. RFIs in Outlook. Compliance in PDFs. Every clause re-typed three times across 11 active projects.

We tried tools. They each solved a slice. None solved the layer underneath. So we built it ourselves. HoistOS is the operating layer. Estimating, RFIs, AR/AP, payroll, org changes, project economics. One stack, real data, audit-ready, on a single source of truth Claude can read end to end.

Now we are productizing it. Two surfaces below. The first is the public scrolophyte: every hard-won AI lesson we banked, packaged so you can install them in your own Claude in eight minutes of scroll. The second is the read-only org dashboard, gated to the team. We did not buy a tool. We built a layer. We are giving you the keys.
